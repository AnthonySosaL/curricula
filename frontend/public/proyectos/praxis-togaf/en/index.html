How it works

  • 1. Open the board: it comes pre-loaded with a worked example, so you instantly see how each phase is filled in.
  • 2. Describe your project: in up to 500 characters. The AI fills in the phases while respecting each one's purpose.
  • 3. Refine: edit Inputs, Steps and Outputs, and reorder with drag and drop.
  • 4. Export: the full board or just inputs/outputs, as a lightweight PDF ready to hand in.

Who it's for

  • Engineering and IS students who need to deliver a TOGAF assignment.
  • Architects and analysts who want a fast first draft of every phase.
  • Teachers looking for a visual way to explain the ADM cycle.

Why the AI doesn't give you filler

Every AI response is validated before it's shown: if a phase comes back incomplete or generic, it's rejected and regenerated instead of being served silently.

Tech stack

  • Backend: ASP.NET Core, C#, Entity Framework Core, SQL Server, JWT, ASP.NET Identity
  • Frontend: Angular 22, Angular Material, Angular CDK (drag & drop), Signals
  • AI: Groq API
  • Export: jsPDF + html2canvas, optimized from ~25 MB down to ~200 KB per PDF

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the full board as a guest. AI generation works without an account too; registering only adds database persistence (up to 3 projects).

Is it free?

Yes, it's an open project for practicing and learning TOGAF.

Is it an official The Open Group tool?

No. It's an independent project based on the TOGAF ADM method.
