How it works

  • 1. Placement test: 64 multiple-choice questions across grammar, vocabulary, reading and listening, from A2 to C1.
  • 2. A clear breakdown: your CEFR level plus which specific areas are solid and which need work. Any area graded under 7/10 is flagged.
  • 3. AI reinforcement: fresh, non-repeated questions for the level and skill where you struggle most.
  • 4. Mini-courses: 8 to 11 slides with explanations and interactive exercises (drag-and-drop, fill-in), plus a mini-quiz.
  • 5. AI coach: a progress diagnostic built from your whole history, not just your latest attempt.

Cambridge-style speaking with an AI examiner

The speaking test is the most nerve-racking part and the hardest one to practice alone. Certis simulates the four typical parts of the exam:

  • Interview: personal questions to break the ice.
  • Long turn: speaking without interruption about a topic.
  • Collaborative task: negotiating and reaching a decision.
  • Discussion: giving and defending opinions on broader ideas.

Who it's for

  • Anyone preparing for B2 First or C1 Advanced who wants to measure their starting point.
  • Professionals who need to prove their level for a remote job.
  • Tutors and schools: it includes an Admin/Tutor panel with per-student tracking and early-warning flags.

FAQ

What is the CEFR level?

The Common European Framework of Reference ranks language ability from A1 to C2. B2 is what many employers ask for; C1 is advanced proficiency.

How long does the placement test take?

About 25 minutes: 64 multiple-choice questions designed to be completed in one sitting.

Is the result an official certificate?

No. It's an assessment and practice tool; official certificates are issued by bodies such as Cambridge.
